ModalAI module reference

Part-number lookup for the ModalAI catalogue, supporting the VOXL 2 Mini notes. Everything a build touches is an M00xx board, and the numbers are the only reliable identifier — the marketing names collide (three different boards are called simply “VOXL 2”).

Compiled 2026-07-31 from the live PCB catalog and the per-board datasheet pages: 85 distinct part numbers, of which 73 are current and 54 have a dedicated page at docs.modalai.com/<part number>/.

The What it is column adds integration context: direct VOXL 2/Mini fit, required carrier or flex, software role, and reasons not to choose a legacy part. Electrical compatibility alone is insufficient for MIPI cameras; a published driver, kernel variant, and camera configuration are also required.

Four things worth knowing before using the catalog:

  • The catalog table is stale. 19 boards have live datasheet pages but do not appear in it at all — including M0135, M0149, M0161, M0166, M0169, M0178, M0186, M0187, M0189 and M0195, which is most of the current sensor and adapter line. Search the docs by part number instead of trusting the table.
  • 2D drawings are rare. The thumbnails in the 2D column link to full-size copies kept in images/modalai/, converted from the ModalAI drawing PDFs or copied from the docs site. Where no drawing is published, three-view hidden-line projections (top / bottom / edge) rendered here from the part’s STEP file stand in — board outline, mounting holes, connectors and silkscreen are accurate, but there are no dimensions and assemblies may include a mating connector or lens. A blank cell means neither exists.
  • Prices are snapshots. Unqualified links are current ModalAI listings; AMOV marks a current exact-SKU AMOV RobotLab reseller listing, while ModalAI archive is the last manufacturer-store capture found and is not a current offer. All amounts are USD, checked 2026-08-06 (archives carry their capture month), before tax and shipping. A single amount is the cheapest exact-part configuration found; qualifiers distinguish board, carrier, kit, EOL, or sold-out stock. A range means configuration options change the price. — means no standalone exact-part public price was found after checking the manufacturer feed/store, distributor catalogs, docs buy links, web search, and the Internet Archive; successor and bundle prices are not substituted.
  • Weights are inconsistent. Some are bare PCB, some include cables or lids (M0030 is listed as “8.24g / 14g with modem + lid”). Treat them as indicative.

Interposers, flexes, adapters

The part of the catalogue that quietly decides the mechanical layout.

Connector families, since the part descriptions assume familiarity with them:

  • DF40 — Hirose 60-pin, 0.4 mm pitch (DF40C-60DP-0.4V(51)). The camera ports on VOXL 2 and the Mini, J6 and J7. Each carries two camera “groups”.
  • AXT — Panasonic P4S board-to-board, 36-pin (AXT336124 board side, AXT436124 mating). The image-sensor connector on VOXL 1 and VOXL Flight, and still the format most sensor modules and flexes terminate in.

“DF40C to AXT” adapters therefore exist to bridge generations: they break one group of signals (CSI lanes, timers, resets) out of a 60-pin DF40 port into a 36-pin AXT connector. M0084 is the Y-variant that fans one port out to two sensors.
